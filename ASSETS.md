# Assets and licenses

- The launcher layouts, schematic preview art, letter icons and social preview graphics are original Atelier Craft assets under `LICENSE`. The localized PNGs in `web/og-*.png` and `web/twitter-*.png` are generated from `scripts/make_social_images.py`.
- `web/fonts/dm-sans-*.ttf` and `web/fonts/fraunces-500.ttf` have their respective Open Font License notices in `web/fonts/`.
- No official ArtCraft screenshots, app icons, wordmarks or app binaries are committed to this repository. Their redistribution rights were not verified.
- Official web app releases are downloaded by a self-hosted instance directly from the upstream projects. They retain their upstream licenses and attribution.

To add a third-party visual later, first record its source URL, exact license and attribution requirements here, and verify that the license permits redistribution in an independent launcher.
